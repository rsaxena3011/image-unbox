    Quality is preserved as stored in the document. Previously compressed images cannot be restored to their earlier quality.Image Unbox · v1.0 · Free & open source

    Good images, out of the file.

    1. Choose your documents.Add one or more .pptx, .docx or .xlsx files. Image Unbox reads embedded image files locally. Microsoft Office does not need to be installed.
    2. Preview and choose.Filter by format or dimensions, inspect an image, and select the files you want. “Select shown” selects the current page. “Select unique” selects one copy of each byte-identical image across the full collection.
    3. Save the originals.Save an individual image or export your selection as a ZIP, organised by source document. An optional CSV index lists file paths, formats, dimensions and sizes.
    What “original” means:the exact image bytes stored inside the document. Extraction does not resize or recompress them. The tool cannot restore detail already lost before or while saving the document. Crops, effects, rotations and slide layouts are not rendered; the stored image may look different from its appearance on a slide.
    Supported:standard, unencrypted PPTX, DOCX and XLSX files. Common raster images preview in the browser. SVG, TIFF, EMF, WMF and some other formats can be saved even when a preview is unavailable. SVG is never rendered here. Large images may show a placeholder to save memory.
    Not supported:PDF, old PPT/DOC/XLS, password-protected or ZIP64 packages, linked images stored outside the document, and converting charts, shapes or SmartArt into pictures. Media from slide masters, hidden slides, headers and footers may be included when it is embedded.
    Limits:500 MB per document, 64 MB per image, up to 2,000 images / 250 MB of extracted image bytes per collection. Use an updated desktop browser. Settings and images are cleared when this tab is closed. No telemetry or external requests.
